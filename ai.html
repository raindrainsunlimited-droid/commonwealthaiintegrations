AI in plain English

Think of it like a good office assistant that never clocks out. You don't need to understand how it works. That's our job. Here's what it looks like day to day:

Common questions

What owners ask us most.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. We set everything up and train you and your team in person until you're comfortable. If you can use a smartphone, you can use what we set up.

Will AI replace my people?

No. It takes the repetitive computer work off their plate, like typing up estimates, adding up hours and calling to confirm appointments, so your people can spend their time on the work that makes you money.

Do I have to switch to new software?

Usually not. We work with the programs you already use and connect them. If something you pay for isn't pulling its weight, we'll tell you and help you replace or cancel it.

Is my information safe?

We don't store your data or your customers' data. Your information stays in your own accounts. For law offices and medical practices, we set things up to fit the privacy rules your office has to follow.

Who decides what gets automated?

You do. We sort every task with you: some run on their own, some wait for you or a team member to approve, and some stay fully in human hands. You choose who can approve what, and you can pause or change anything at any time.

Does AI make mistakes?

It can, which is why we set it up so you stay in charge. Anything important, like an estimate or an invoice, can wait for your OK before it goes out, and we test everything on real work before handing it over.

What does it actually look like day to day?

Estimates you talk through from the truck come back written up. Customers get reminder texts on their own. Hours are totaled and payroll runs itself. New leads hear back in minutes. At Rain Drains, that took Bryson's computer work from 20 hours a week to 2.
