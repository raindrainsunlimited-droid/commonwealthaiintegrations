What we do

We run the office work and help bring in the customers.

We build around how you already run things. No starting over with new software you don't want.

Run the office

Scheduling and planning ahead

Jobs get booked, crews get assigned and customers get reminders without a pile of phone calls. You can see busy weeks and slow weeks coming, so you can plan your crews and your cash ahead of time.

Estimates, invoices and customer updates

Clean estimate templates, invoices that go out when the job's done, and payment reminders that follow on their own. Customers get a text or email when their estimate is ready, the crew is on the way or the job is done.

Payroll and bookkeeping

Crew hours totaled and ready for payroll, then payroll runs itself. Receipts sorted and books kept up to date. Each month you get a simple profit and loss summary: what came in, what went out and what you kept.

Supplies and materials

Know what you have, what's running low and what each job needs. Orders are ready for your supplier before the truck comes up short.

Your customer list, organized

We set up or clean up the customer software you already have, with every customer's info, past jobs and photos brought into one place.

Phone calls and follow-up

Missed calls get a text back, new leads get an answer fast, and past customers get a check-in when it's time for service again.

Email inbox help

Your inbox gets sorted for you, the urgent emails get flagged, and replies are drafted for you to check and send.

Add-on

Estimates from the field

Talk through or jot down the job from the truck, add pictures or drawings, and the estimate is written up for you to review and send.

Add-on

Custom-built customer system

When nothing on the market fits your business, we build a customer system made for the way you work.

Bring in more customers

Getting found online

Show up when people search Google, or ask AI tools like ChatGPT, for someone in your trade nearby. We tune your Google listing, website and reviews so the calls come to you. (You may have heard this called SEO and AEO.) Included with every website we build.

Your website, built and kept up

A clean website that works on phones and brings in calls, set up to get found on Google and AI tools from day one. We can keep it up to date for you, so you're not paying an agency every time you need a change.

Social media, done for you

Regular posts that show off your work: before-and-after photos, happy customers, crews on the job. Led by Maddox, who built his own following past 400,000 people.

Ads that bring in calls

Photo and video ads on Facebook and Instagram, aimed at homeowners and businesses in your service area. We track every call and lead so you know exactly what your ad money brings back.

Cold outreach and winning back past customers

New work and repeat work, together. We reach out to builders, property managers and other businesses that could send you steady jobs, and track every reply. It's how Rain Drains landed new partnerships. Your past customers already trust you, so we also set up check-ins, service reminders and referral requests so they come back and send their friends.
